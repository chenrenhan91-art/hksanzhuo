# Design QA

final result: blocked

The source and prototype home screens were captured in `source.png` and `local.png` and combined in `comparison.png`. The captures have different heights (1440×810 and 1440×900), so this is not a normalized comparison. Desktop composition, source furniture imagery, Eina typography and product grid were inspected; the source logo was deliberately replaced with company text. The original checkout remained behind security verification. The requested frontend-only scope omits backend checkout and full catalog. Mobile was viewed in-browser but a same-size side-by-side capture has not been completed.

The hero background mismatch was corrected by matching the image background. The local browser was tested for product detail, cart addition and totals, and USD/HKD switching. A fresh browser tab reported no console errors. Further normalized desktop and mobile comparison is needed before claiming 1:1 visual fidelity.
